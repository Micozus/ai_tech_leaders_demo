# Payout status

Whether the mentor can be paid through Stripe Connect.

Backlog: E02-S05 (#24), 1.1

## What it is for

A mentor must know why they are not yet paid and what to do.

## When to use

- The mentor home in 1.1 (D13).

## When not to use

| Situation               | Use instead    |
| ----------------------- | -------------- |
| The payment of a mentee | Payment status |
