# Cancellation notice

The cancellation rule in words, for this booking.

## What it is for

R09: cancelling is free up to 24 hours before the session, later the fee is forfeited. The notice computes which applies.

## When to use

- The payment step and the cancel confirmation.

## When not to use

| Situation        | Use instead |
| ---------------- | ----------- |
| A reminder email | Not built   |
